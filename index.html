Recurrence Risk Calculator for NMIBC Patients after Intravesical BCG Therapy

This tool estimates 1- and 3-year recurrence-free survival (RFS) probabilities based on the nomogram developed by Lu et al. It is intended for research purposes and requires external validation before clinical use.

Please enter positive values for both cell densities.

Probabilities were derived from the Cox model: S(t) = S0(t)^exp(lp), where lp = 0.949 × (Recurrence history) + 0.910 × (Riskscore), and the Riskscore = exp(0.271 × log2(CD4) + 0.273 × log2(CD68s)) . CD4 denotes the total CD4+ cell density in the whole tissue, and CD68s denotes the CD68+ cell density in the stromal compartment (cells/mm²).
